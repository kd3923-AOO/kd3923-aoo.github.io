Energy & cooldown rules
  • Energy Points build up on their own; enough of them makes one Energy Bar.
  • The team that is behind on score gains energy faster, depending on the gap.
  • Some skills also generate energy (Stocking Up, Energetic).
  • Each skill has its own cooldown. Skills with a 3,600 s cooldown are effectively once per match.
  • Running Charge, War Drums and Mystical Formation end as soon as an affected troop enters a building. Cast them in the open field.
